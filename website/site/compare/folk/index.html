Hermit CRM vs folk

Prices and features checked on 23 September 2026.

The short answer

Choose folk if you work with a small team, manage many relationships and want contacts enriched and captured from LinkedIn and your mailbox for you. Choose Hermit CRM if you work alone and want your companies, contacts and conversations as plain files on your own computer, with AI tools reading them directly. Hermit CRM is free; folk has a 14-day trial and starts at €22 a member a month billed yearly.

When folk is the better choice

  • A few people share the same contacts and pipelines.
  • You want contacts enriched with emails and company data for you.
  • You want mail campaigns and follow-ups sent from the CRM.
  • You want a mobile app and a vendor that supports you.
  • You work on Windows.

When Hermit CRM is the better choice

  • You work alone and want no subscription.
  • You want your contacts and conversations as files on your own disk.
  • You want an AI agent to read and update your CRM through files and a CLI, and every change it makes kept in git.
  • You want to add a contact from a LinkedIn profile without installing an extension: Hermit CRM uses a bookmarklet that reads the page in your own tab.
  • You want message drafts in your own wording, in English, German, Dutch or French.

Hermit CRM is a free, open-source CRM for founders, freelancers and anyone doing their own sales. It runs on your Mac or Linux computer and keeps every company, contact and conversation as a Markdown file in a git folder on your own disk. A local web app gives you a pipeline board, a calendar of next steps, message drafts and reports. AI tools such as Claude Code, Codex or Claude Desktop can read and update the pipeline through the files, a small CLI or the built-in MCP server. It is alpha software under the Apache License 2.0, with no warranty and no support.

Moving from folk to Hermit CRM

  1. In folk, export your contacts and companies as CSV.
  2. In Hermit CRM, choose Import a spreadsheet on the home page and upload each file. It shows a preview and writes nothing until you confirm.
  3. Columns Hermit CRM has no field for are kept in the notes. Add fields in Settings if you want them as columns.

Questions

Is Hermit CRM an alternative to folk?

For one person, yes. folk is a hosted relationship CRM for small teams, with enrichment and mail sync. Hermit CRM is a free local CRM for one person that keeps the data in plain files on your computer.

Can Hermit CRM add contacts from LinkedIn like folk?

Yes, one at a time. Its bookmarklet turns a LinkedIn profile you are looking at into a filled-in contact form, read in your own browser tab. It does not look up email addresses for you.

Does Hermit CRM enrich contacts?

It fills in company details from a company's website for free, or through an AI command line tool you already use, such as Claude or Codex. It has no database of contact data of its own.
