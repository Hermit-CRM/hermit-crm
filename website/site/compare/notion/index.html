Hermit CRM vs Notion

Prices and features checked on 23 September 2026.

The short answer

Choose Notion if you already work in Notion, share it with others, and a database with a board view is all the CRM you need. Choose Hermit CRM if you want a CRM that does CRM work for you: mail logged on the right contact by BCC, a follow-up radar for unanswered threads, meeting briefs and reports, with the data as plain files on your own computer. Both are free for one person.

When Notion is the better choice

  • You already run your notes, docs and projects in Notion and want sales next to them.
  • Others work in the same workspace.
  • A list of leads with a status column is all you need.
  • You want apps on Windows and on your phone.

When Hermit CRM is the better choice

  • You want mail and meetings logged on the right company without copying them by hand.
  • You want to see which threads wait for your answer, and which went quiet.
  • You want a brief before each meeting, reports on your funnel and which messages get answers.
  • You want the data on your own disk, as Markdown files in git, not in a workspace in the cloud.
  • You want an AI agent to read and update your pipeline from your own computer.

Hermit CRM is a free, open-source CRM for founders, freelancers and anyone doing their own sales. It runs on your Mac or Linux computer and keeps every company, contact and conversation as a Markdown file in a git folder on your own disk. A local web app gives you a pipeline board, a calendar of next steps, message drafts and reports. AI tools such as Claude Code, Codex or Claude Desktop can read and update the pipeline through the files, a small CLI or the built-in MCP server. It is alpha software under the Apache License 2.0, with no warranty and no support.

Moving from Notion to Hermit CRM

  1. In Notion, export your CRM database as CSV.
  2. In Hermit CRM, choose Import a spreadsheet on the home page and upload the file. It shows a preview and writes nothing until you confirm.
  3. Columns Hermit CRM has no field for are kept in the notes. Add fields in Settings if you want them as columns.

Questions

Is Notion good enough as a CRM?

For a short list of leads, often yes. It becomes work when you want mail logged on contacts, reminders for follow-ups, and reports: in Notion you build and maintain those yourself. Hermit CRM has them built in.

Both keep Markdown. What is the difference?

Notion stores your pages in its cloud and can export them as Markdown. Hermit CRM stores them as Markdown from the start, in a folder on your computer, and commits every change to git.

Can I move my Notion CRM to Hermit CRM?

Yes. Export the Notion database as CSV and import it in Hermit CRM. It shows a preview first, and columns it has no field for are kept as notes.
