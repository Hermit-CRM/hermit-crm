Hermit CRM vs Pipedrive

Prices and features checked on 23 September 2026.

The short answer

Choose Pipedrive if you run a sales team, want automations and email sequences, or need a mobile app. Choose Hermit CRM if you sell on your own, want a pipeline board without a subscription, and want the data as plain files on your own computer that AI tools can read. Hermit CRM is free; Pipedrive has a 14-day trial and starts at €14 a seat a month billed yearly.

When Pipedrive is the better choice

  • A team works the same pipeline and you want goals and reports per person.
  • You want automations and email sequences built in.
  • You need a mobile app for calls and meetings on the road.
  • You want a vendor that supports you when something breaks.
  • You work on Windows.

When Hermit CRM is the better choice

  • You sell on your own and a subscription per seat is hard to justify.
  • You want the pipeline in files on your own disk, not in a vendor's cloud.
  • You work with Claude Code, Codex or another AI agent and want it to read and update deals without an API key.
  • You want a follow-up radar, message drafts in four languages and reports, without paying for a higher plan.
  • You want every change kept in git, so you can see and undo it.

Hermit CRM is a free, open-source CRM for founders, freelancers and anyone doing their own sales. It runs on your Mac or Linux computer and keeps every company, contact and conversation as a Markdown file in a git folder on your own disk. A local web app gives you a pipeline board, a calendar of next steps, message drafts and reports. AI tools such as Claude Code, Codex or Claude Desktop can read and update the pipeline through the files, a small CLI or the built-in MCP server. It is alpha software under the Apache License 2.0, with no warranty and no support.

Moving from Pipedrive to Hermit CRM

  1. In Pipedrive, export your organisations and people as CSV or Excel files.
  2. In Hermit CRM, choose Import a spreadsheet on the home page and upload each file. It recognises Pipedrive's column names, shows a preview, and writes nothing until you confirm.
  3. Set the stage of each company on the pipeline board. Email history and activities stay in Pipedrive.

Questions

Is there a free alternative to Pipedrive for one person?

Yes. Hermit CRM is free and open source and has a pipeline board from prospect to won, a follow-up radar, tasks and reports. It is for one person on a Mac or Linux computer; Pipedrive is for teams, has no free plan and runs in the cloud.

Can I import my Pipedrive data into Hermit CRM?

Organisations and people, yes. Export them from Pipedrive as CSV or Excel and import the files in Hermit CRM, which recognises Pipedrive's column names. Deals, activities and mail history do not come across.

Does Hermit CRM send email sequences like Pipedrive?

No. Hermit CRM drafts messages in your wording, in English, German, Dutch or French, and you send them yourself from your own mail. It logs what you send and whether you got an answer.
