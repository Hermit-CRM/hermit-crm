A CRM for one.

Hermit CRM keeps every company, contact and conversation as a plain text file on your Mac. A local web app gives you a pipeline, a calendar, message drafts and dashboards. Your AI tools can work in it too.

Install with your AI

  1. Open the folder in your AI coding tool: Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI or another.
  2. Send it this message:

Read INSTALL.md and set up Hermit CRM for me.

It checks your Mac, installs what is missing, asks your name and email addresses, and starts the app. Passwords and calendar links you type into the app's Settings page yourself, so they stay on your Mac.

Prefer the terminal? Install by hand.

The idea

One company, one file, one commit.

The file behind the Acme card:

~/crm/companies/acme/company.md
---
name: Acme BV
slug: acme
website: https://acme.example.com
country: NL
source: other
stage: prospect
stage_changed: 2026-09-17
...

Add a company in the app or from the terminal. Either way it becomes this file, and one commit records it.

What it does

Pipeline
A board from prospect to won, plus a follow-up radar for unanswered threads.
Calendar
Overdue and due-today next steps, a month view, and a brief before each meeting.
Contacts
Companies and people in sortable tables, and one timeline per company of every email, call and meeting.
Messages
Outreach drafts in English, German, Dutch or French, in your wording, and the outcome of every message.
Dashboards
Weekly activity, funnel and conversion, time in stage, and which messages get answers.

Local. It runs on your Mac. Your data is a folder of Markdown files in git, on your own disk.

AI-native. Plain files, a small CLI and an MCP server, so Claude Code, Codex or Claude Desktop can read your pipeline and log a call. Ask Hermit answers questions on every page.

Configurable. One commented settings file, your own message wording, and any fields you add are kept.

Questions

Where does my data live?

In a folder on your Mac, ~/crm unless you choose another. Every company, contact and conversation is a Markdown file, and every change is a git commit, so any mistake is one git revert away.

Does anything leave my Mac?

Only what you send. A private git backup, if you add one. The AI tool you ask, when you use Ask Hermit or enrichment. A once-a-day version check with no identifiers, which you can turn off.

Do I need to be a developer?

No. Your AI tool runs the commands and tells you in plain words what it is doing. On a fresh Mac you click Install once in an Apple dialog.

Which AI tools work?

For the install, any AI coding tool that can read a file and run commands: Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI and others. Afterwards, Claude Desktop, ChatGPT, Cursor or any other MCP client can read your pipeline and log interactions.

Does it work with my email and calendar?

Yes, without OAuth. BCC or forward mail to an address such as you+crm@gmail.com and it is logged on the right contact. Past meetings come in from your calendar's secret ICS link (Google, Outlook or iCloud). You type the app password and the link into the Settings page yourself, so they never pass through an AI chat.

Can my team use it?

No. Hermit CRM is built for one person. If you need a team CRM, use one of the others.

What does it cost?

Nothing. It is open source under the MIT license.
