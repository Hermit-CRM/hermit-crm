Hermit CRM in numbers

Hermit CRM is built in public, so here are its numbers: visits to this site, downloads, GitHub stars and AI tools reading the docs. They are updated every day. The site sets no cookies and keeps no IP addresses.

The numbers are not published yet. Come back tomorrow.

How we count

  • Arrivals are visits that began somewhere else: a link, a bookmark, the address bar. A click from one page of this site to another is not a new arrival.
  • Downloads are installs by pip or uv from PyPI (not CI, mirrors or browsers), release files from GitHub, and complete downloads from this site (not bots, and not cut off halfway).
  • AI fetches are pages read by a named AI crawler or assistant, such as ClaudeBot or GPTBot.
  • Anything seen fewer than 3 times in a list (a site that sent visitors, a page, a country) is counted under Other, because one visit can point at one person.
  • What we set aside (bots, scripts, CI, our own tests) is listed at the end, as totals.
