Extension: save a page to the CRM

Drag this to your bookmarks bar. On any company website or LinkedIn company page, click it: Hermit CRM reads the page and opens a filled-in new-company form. On a LinkedIn profile you are logged in to, it reads the page you are looking at and opens a filled-in new-contact form.

What it reads

A company page: the website, the LinkedIn company page it links to, the country, an employee-count hint and a one-line description, from the page's own metadata, which your machine fetches directly.

A LinkedIn profile: the name, the current role, the company and its LinkedIn page, the location, and the email when Contact info is open, read in your own tab. Nothing is fetched and nothing goes to LinkedIn. If you took this bookmarklet before, take it again: the old one reads profiles logged out, which gives very little.

Nothing is written until you press Create on the form it opens, and nothing is sent anywhere but this Hermit CRM. More in Help.

Hermit CRM is serving on {{ base }}, so the bookmarklet points there. If you later run serve on a different host or port, come back to this page and take the bookmarklet again.
