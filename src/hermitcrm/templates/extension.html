Save a LinkedIn conversation

Hermit: conversation ← drag me too

Open the conversation in LinkedIn, either on the Messaging page or in the chat bubble that opens from a profile’s Message button, and click the bookmark. Hermit CRM opens with the conversation read, matched to a contact and checked against what you have already logged. Review it, then press Import: each new message becomes a LinkedIn interaction, in one step. Nothing is fetched and nothing goes to LinkedIn: the bookmark reads the conversation in your own tab and hands it to this Hermit CRM, and nothing is written until you press Import. It reads only the messages the page has already loaded, so scroll up in LinkedIn first if you want older ones.

A word on LinkedIn’s rules. LinkedIn’s User Agreement (8.2) forbids automated access to LinkedIn and software that scrapes or copies its pages, and it names browser plug-ins and add-ons in that list. This bookmark is the mildest form of it: it runs only when you click it, only on the conversation you have open, it reads only what that page already shows (it never scrolls, clicks, opens other conversations or asks LinkedIn for anything), and the messages are your own. It is still not something LinkedIn has approved, so use it knowing that.

What it reads

  • A company page: the website, its LinkedIn page, the country, an employee-count hint and a one-line description, from the page's own metadata (your machine fetches it directly).
  • A LinkedIn profile you are logged in to: the name, current role, company, location, and the email when Contact info is open. It is read in your own tab: nothing is fetched and nothing goes to LinkedIn.

Nothing is saved until you press Create on the form, and nothing is sent anywhere but this Hermit CRM. More in Help
